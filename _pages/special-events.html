2025 OpenVT Multicellular Modelling Workshop:

July 13th 2025
We are pleased to announce the 2025 OpenVT Workshop on Multiscale Model Sharing and Reproducibility, being held as a satellite meeting of the Society of Mathematical Biology (SMB) meeting, on July 13th 2025 at the University of Alberta, Edmonton, Alberta Canada. This one day workshop will contain tutorials on popular multicellular modelling tools and a multicellular model reproducibility hackathon. For more details and to sign-up see: https://forms.gle/2uBjsVUMXEwngVwF6.
