title: "On the study of Complete Electrode Model for Electroencephalography"
abstract: "In this work, we study the applicability of the Complete Electrode Model (CEM) to Electroencephalography (EEG). EEG is a non-invasive imaging technique where it aims to localize cerebral sources generating the measured EEG signals. An existence and uniqueness result of this model is proved. Preliminary numerical implementation are also done."
